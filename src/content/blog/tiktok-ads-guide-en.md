# The Complete Guide to TikTok UGC Ads in 2025

TikTok has revolutionized how brands communicate with consumers. With over 1 billion active users, the platform cannot be ignored.

## What Makes a Great TikTok UGC Ad?

The best TikTok ads don't look like ads. They look like organic content. That's the secret.

### Elements of a high-converting TikTok UGC:

**The Hook (first 3 seconds)**
This is the most important element. If you don't capture the viewer within 3 seconds, they're gone. Good hooks:
- Ask a provocative question
- Show a surprising result
- Start in the middle of an action

**The Middle (social proof and benefits)**
Here you show the product and explain the benefits. Keep it concrete and user-friendly.

**CTA (call to action)**
Be direct: "Click here", "Buy now", "See more". Vague CTA = poor conversion.

## Technical Requirements
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Duration: 15-60 seconds
- Sound: Always include – 80% watch with sound
