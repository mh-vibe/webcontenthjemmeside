# How DTC Brands Use UGC to Scale Their Ads

User Generated Content (UGC) is not just a buzzword – it's one of the most effective advertising strategies for direct-to-consumer brands today.

## Why Does UGC Work?

Consumers trust other consumers. Studies show that 92% of people trust recommendations from real people more than traditional ads. UGC content performs better because:

- It feels genuine and authentic
- It shows the product in real situations
- It creates social proof
- It's cheaper to produce than traditional ad content

## Getting Started with UGC

1. **Define your audience** – Who uses your product? Find creators who match your demographic.
2. **Write a good brief** – Give creators clear guidelines but also freedom to express themselves.
3. **Test many variations** – UGC is about volume. Test 10-20 creatives and scale the best ones.
4. **Analyze and optimize** – Look at metrics like hook rate, hold rate, and CTR.

## Platform vs. DIY

You can find creators yourself or use a platform like WebContent. A platform gives you:
- Access to thousands of pre-qualified creators
- Faster production
- Lower risk
- Rights included

Start with a few test videos and see the difference for yourself.
