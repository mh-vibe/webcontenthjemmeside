# Gogoevent: -70% Lower CPI with UGC Ads

## About Gogoevent

Gogoevent is an app for local events and social experiences in Scandinavia. They help users discover and book local events, from gastronomic experiences to sporting events.

**Industry:** Apps & Digital  
**Country:** Denmark  
**Team:** Marketing & Growth

## The Challenge

Gogoevent faced a classic challenge: high ad costs and declining ROAS on Meta and Google. Their traditional production-based ads were no longer working, and they needed a new approach.

Specifically, they struggled with:
- CPI (cost per install) was too high to scale profitably
- Ad fatigue – the same creatives over and over
- Lack of authentic content that resonated with young, social users

## The Solution

Gogoevent started working with WebContent to produce UGC ads from real users. They launched campaigns with:

- 15 unique UGC videos from 8 different creators
- TikTok-optimized hooks focusing on FOMO and social experience
- Voice over variations for quick A/B testing

## The Results

The results spoke for themselves:

| Metric | Before WebContent | After WebContent |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|
| CPI | €6.50 | €1.95 |
| CTR | 1.2% | 3.8% |
| ROAS | 1.8x | 4.2x |

**-70% lower CPI** – From €6.50 to €1.95 per install.

**+50% higher UGC budget** – When the results came in, Gogoevent immediately increased their budget for UGC content.
